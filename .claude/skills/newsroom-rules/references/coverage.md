## Tier B: important matches only (big games, finals, knockouts, Arab clubs/players)

**Club competitions**
- Europe: UEFA Champions League, Europa League, Conference League, UEFA Super Cup
- Africa: CAF Champions League, CAF Confederation Cup, CAF Super Cup
- Asia: AFC Champions League Elite, AFC Champions League Two
- Arab: Arab Club Champions Cup
- World: FIFA Club World Cup, FIFA Intercontinental Cup
- Domestic cups of the Tier A leagues (FA Cup, Copa del Rey, Coppa Italia, King's Cup, Egypt Cup) and their super cups: from quarter-finals, or when a big club plays

**National teams**
- Arab national teams: all official matches (Egypt, Saudi Arabia, Morocco, Algeria, Tunisia, Qatar, Iraq, Jordan, UAE, and others)
- European national teams: big matches only (e.g. France, England, Spain, Germany, Italy, Portugal, Netherlands, Belgium, Croatia)
- Brazil and Argentina: all official matches
- Tournaments: FIFA World Cup and qualifiers, AFCON and qualifiers, AFC Asian Cup, FIFA Arab Cup, UEFA Euro and Nations League, Copa América, Olympic football; youth World Cups only when an Arab team goes far

## What counts as "important" (Tier B)

Cover a Tier B match if at least one is true:
1. An Arab club, or Arab national team, is playing.
2. A notable Arab player is involved (e.g. Salah, Marmoush, Hakimi, Mahrez, Brahim Díaz, Amrabat).
3. It is a final, semi-final, or knock-out tie, or decides a title or qualification.
4. A heavyweight clash: two of Real Madrid, Barcelona, Man City, Liverpool, Arsenal, Man United, Chelsea, Bayern, PSG, Inter, Milan, Juventus, Napoli, Atlético; or two top national teams.
5. Brazil or Argentina play.
6. A record or historic moment (e.g. a record goal, a first-ever qualification).

Everything else in Tier B: result only, included in a round-up, or skipped.
