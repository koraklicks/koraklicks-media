# Platform specs

## Facebook
- Image: 1080×1350 (feed) or 1600×900 (link-style). Video: 1080×1920 reels or 1080×1080.
- Caption: hook line, 1–3 short lines, source line. Put links in the first comment.
- Best for: full news, results with stats, match-day threads (one post updated during a match).
- Cadence guide: 6–12 posts/day; group several small results into one round-up.

## Instagram
- Feed: 1080×1350. Stories/Reels: 1080×1920; keep the top 200 px and bottom 300 px free of text.
- Caption: first line is the hook (shown before "more"), then 2–3 lines, source, 3–6 hashtags.
- Stories for live updates (goal, HT, FT); feed for big news and final results; carousels for round-ups.
- No clickable links in captions; say «اللينك في البايو» only if there is one.

## X (Twitter)
- Text ≤ 280 characters incl. source. Image 1600×900.
- Fastest platform: breaking news and every live goal go here first.
- Use one thread per match: kickoff → goals → HT → FT. Reply to own tweet to keep the thread.
- 1–2 hashtags max (e.g. #الدوري_المصري #دوري_روشن).

## TikTok
- Vertical 1080×1920, 7–30 s. Hook text on screen in the first 2 seconds.
- Formats: animated result card, "3 facts in 15 seconds", top goals of the round (only with footage the owner has rights to; otherwise use graphics and text, never re-uploaded broadcast clips).
- Caption: one line + 3–5 hashtags (#كورة #football #الدوري_الانجليزي …). Credit source in caption.
- Trending sounds only from TikTok's commercial-use library.
