# Weekly growth and revenue review

1. Load `newsroom-rules` and `references/growth-playbook.md`.
2. **Data:** collect last week's analytics from Metricool (blogId `7358209`, `getAnalyticsDataByMetrics`). If a platform is not connected, ask the owner for its CSV export (and tell them exactly where to download it), and continue with what exists. Never estimate missing numbers.
3. **growth-analyst:** scorecard, top/bottom posts, patterns, experiment result, 3–5 actions; update playbook sections 6 and 8.
4. **monetization-manager:** programme progress tracker (thresholds checked on official pages, with dates), revenue by stream, sponsor pipeline, 3 revenue actions.
5. **BOSS:** merge into next week's plan: content mix changes, the one experiment per platform, posting times, YouTube topics, and any rule updates for the writer, creative and publishers.
6. Deliver one Arabic report to the owner: «📈 النمو» → «💰 الأرباح» → «🧪 تجربة الأسبوع الجاي» → «✅ القرارات». Ask the owner to approve any change that affects money, sponsors or brand rules.
