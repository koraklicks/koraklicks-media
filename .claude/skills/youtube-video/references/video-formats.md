# The three Kora Klicks YouTube formats

All three are **motion-graphics videos**: animated cards, scores, tables and text in the brand
look. They never contain match footage or broadcast clips (copyright strikes / Content ID).
«ملخص» here means a summary of events, not video highlights.

## 1. ملخص الدوريات الأوروبية الكبرى (europe-roundup)

- When: once a week after the round ends (usually Monday or Tuesday). Add midweek editions in Champions League weeks.
- Length: 6–10 min.
- Structure:
  1. `intro`: «ملخص الجولة [n] · الدوريات الأوروبية الكبرى»
  2. For each league, in this order: Premier League, La Liga, Serie A
     - `section` (league name + round)
     - `result` for the 2–4 biggest matches (scorers, a `note` for the match of the round)
     - `list` «باقي النتايج» with the other results (one line each: «الفريق أ 2-1 الفريق ب»)
     - `table` top 4–5
  3. `stat` of the week (record, top scorer total…)
  4. `list` «ركن العرب 🌍»: what Arab players did (goals, assists, minutes)
  5. Champions League corner when relevant
  6. `outro`

## 2. ملخص الدوري المصري والسعودي (egypt-saudi-roundup)

- When: once a week after both rounds end. Extra edition after a big derby (Al Ahly–Zamalek, Al Hilal–Al Nassr, etc.).
- Length: 5–8 min.
- Structure: `intro` → Egyptian league (`section`, key `result`s, `list` other results, `table`) → Saudi Pro League (same) → `stat` → `list` «نجوم الجولة» → CAF/AFC club news if relevant → `outro`.

## 3. أهم الأخبار (top-news)

- When: 2–3 times a week (e.g. Sunday, Wednesday, Friday), or a special after a huge news day.
- Length: 3–6 min.
- Structure: `intro` «أهم [n] أخبار في الكورة» → countdown of 6–10 `headline` scenes (`index` from n down to 1, biggest story last) mixing transfers, injuries (official only), coach changes, records, and national-team news (Arab teams, Brazil, Argentina, big European teams) → `outro` with a question for comments.
- **Only CONFIRMED stories.** An UNCONFIRMED story may appear only if the owner approved it, phrased as «حسب [source]..».

## YouTube metadata (the writer delivers it with every script)

- **Title** ≤ 70 characters, Egyptian dialect, honest, e.g. «ملخص الجولة 8 من الدوري الإنجليزي والليجا والكالتشيو | كورة كليكس»
- **Description**: 2-line summary, then **chapters** with timestamps (`00:00 المقدمة` …) built from scene durations, then «المصادر:» with every source used, then hashtags (3–5).
- **Tags**: 10–15 (league names in Arabic and English, team names, «ملخص», «كورة كليكس»).
- **Thumbnail**: the render's thumbnail frame, or the creative's custom 1280×720 thumbnail (big score or face-free headline, ≤ 5 words, brand colours).
- Category: Sports. Language: Arabic. Not made for kids.
